I am a textile laboratory and quality management professional with a strong academic foundation in Physics and Mathematics and extensive experience in the textile industry.

My career began at Gul Ahmed Textile Mills, where I progressed from Management Trainee to Senior Associate Chemist over six years. I subsequently held technical, laboratory, R&D, and quality control management positions at several established textile organizations in Pakistan and Bangladesh.

My expertise includes laboratory operations, physical and performance fabric testing, chemical research and development, instrumental color matching using Datacolor systems, manual shade matching, quality assurance, troubleshooting, and process improvement. At M&N Textiles, I have worked in laboratory management since October 2011, covering laboratory activities and quality assurance.

My approach combines scientific understanding, technical accuracy, practical experience, and continuous improvement to help textile organizations achieve consistent product quality and customer satisfaction. My mission is to share practical textile knowledge and help professionals and organizations strengthen their laboratory capabilities, testing processes, and quality management systems.
